南加州首届高校立直麻将团队赛 The First SoCal Collegiate Riichi Mahjong Team Tournament

五所南加州高校组队出战，在独创的"东西死斗"赛制下展开激烈对决。 不同于传统个人赛——队友同桌作战，策略与配合缺一不可。

Five SoCal university teams clash under our original "East-West Deathmatch" rules. Unlike solo tournaments, your teammate sits at the same table — teamwork and strategy are everything.
赛事亮点 · Highlights

不只是麻将，更是团队博弈 Not Just Mahjong — It's Team Strategy

🀄

2v2 团队赛制

每桌 4 人来自两支队伍，队友并肩作战。每轮出场 4 人分两桌，跨桌合计决胜负。

2v2 Team Format — Teammates at the same table. 4 players per round across 2 tables, combined scores decide the winner.
⚔️

东西死斗

和牌不加分，只扣对手点数。攻防完全倒转，每一手都是对敌人的直接打击。

East-West Deathmatch — Winning hands don't increase your points, they only deduct from opponents. Pure offense.
🔥

满贯缚

只有满贯及以上的和牌才真正扣分。小牌当流局处理——要赢，就赢得漂亮。

Mangan Restriction — Only Mangan+ hands actually count. Sub-Mangan wins are treated as draws. Go big or go home.
🎴

开立直

允许明牌立直——公开手牌额外 +2 翻。高风险，高回报，观赏性拉满。

Open Riichi — Declare riichi with your hand revealed for +2 han. Maximum risk, maximum reward, maximum spectacle.
